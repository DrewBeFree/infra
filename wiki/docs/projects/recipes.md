## How to Use

Recipes is a searchable family recipe collection at [food.drewbefree.com](https://food.drewbefree.com).

### Browsing Recipes

- The home page shows a **card grid** of all recipes, organized by category
- Each card shows the recipe name, category, and a preview image
- Click any card to open the recipe — most link out to the original source site

### Searching

Use the **search bar** to filter recipes by name or ingredient. The grid filters in real time as you type.

### Filtering by Category

Click a **category tag** (e.g., Dinner, Desserts, Breakfast) to filter the grid to that category only. Click again to clear the filter.

### Adding a Recipe (Admin)

Recipes are admin-editable with the shared password.

1. Click **Admin** and enter the password
2. Click **Add Recipe**
3. Fill in: name, category, URL (link to the original recipe), and optional image URL
4. Click **Save** — the recipe appears in the grid immediately

### Editing or Removing a Recipe (Admin)

In admin mode, each card shows **Edit** and **Delete** buttons.

- **Edit** — update the name, category, URL, or image
- **Delete** — removes the recipe from the collection (no undo, so be sure)

### Tips

- Use consistent category names so filters work cleanly (e.g., always "Dinner" not "Dinners")
- If a linked recipe site goes down, update the URL to an archived version or a similar recipe
