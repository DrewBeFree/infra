## How to Use

Recap Viewer displays structured meeting summaries generated by the recap-agents pipeline. Open it at [recap.drewbefree.com](https://recap.drewbefree.com).

### Loading a Recap

Recaps are loaded from your recap-agents history. Use the **History** panel in the left sidebar to browse past meetings — click any entry to load it.

### Sections

Each recap is organized into sections, all reachable from the left sidebar:

| Section | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| **Recap** | One-paragraph summary of the meeting |
| **Agenda** | Topics covered, in order |
| **Action Items** | Assigned tasks with owner and deadline |
| **Questions** | Open questions raised but not yet resolved |
| **Notes** | Raw detail and context from the meeting |
| **Follow-up** | Highlights and items to revisit next meeting |

The sidebar badge on **Action Items** and **Questions** shows the count at a glance.

### Resources Library

The **Library** section in the sidebar lets you attach reference documents to a recap — links, files, or notes relevant to the meeting topic. Click **+** to add a resource.

### Navigation

- Use the sidebar links to jump directly to any section
- On mobile, tap the hamburger menu (☰) to open the sidebar
- The stats bar at the top shows key metrics for the loaded recap

### Tips

- Run the recap-agents pipeline after any meeting to generate a fresh recap
- Action items with no owner assigned show up flagged — assign them before closing the recap
- Use the Questions section as a running list to address at the start of the next meeting
