## How to Use

Linksy tracks real-time scores and wagers for a casual golf round.

### Starting a Round

1. Open [linksy.drewbefree.com](https://linksy.drewbefree.com)
2. Create a new round — enter player names and the wager amounts for each bet type
3. Configure which wagers are active: **Skins**, **Birdies**, **Greenies**, **Sandies**
4. Set a per-hole multiplier if using double/triple holes
5. Click **Start Round**

### Entering Scores

- After each hole, the host enters scores for all players via the **Scorecard** panel
- Scores are color-coded: eagle (gold), birdie (yellow), par (white), bogey+ (red)
- Wagers are calculated automatically as scores come in

### Wager Types

| Wager | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **Skins** | Lowest score on a hole wins the skin. Ties carry over to the next hole. |
| **Birdies** | Each birdie or better pays out from all other players. |
| **Greenies** | Closest to the pin on par-3s (must make par or better to collect). |
| **Sandies** | Par or better from a bunker pays out from other players. |

### Leaderboard

The live leaderboard updates every 5 seconds for all players — everyone can open the URL on their phone and watch in real time.

Net winnings/losses are shown per player, updated hole by hole.

### Finishing the Round

1. Enter the final hole scores
2. Click **End Round** — final standings and payouts are displayed
3. Review who owes whom before closing

### Tips

- Share the URL with players so everyone can follow the leaderboard on their phones
- Carryover skins accumulate and show visually as a growing pot
- The host is the only one who can enter scores
