## How to Use

RV Maintenance tracks scheduled upkeep for the 2019 Thor Motor Coach Freedom Elite 26HE.

### Dashboard

The dashboard shows:
- **Vehicle info** — make, model, year, and current mileage
- **Health score ring** — an overall maintenance health score based on overdue and upcoming items
- **Coming due panel** — items due within the next 30 days
- **Recent activity** — last 5 completed maintenance sessions

### Maintenance Areas

The app tracks 12 areas. Click any area to expand its checklist:

| Area | Example items |
| --- | --- |
| Winterize | Drain water lines, add antifreeze to traps |
| Generator | Oil change, air filter, load test |
| Battery | Charge level check, terminal cleaning |
| Roof | Lap sealant inspection, AC gasket |
| Engine | Oil, coolant, belts, filters |
| Water System | Tank sanitize, pump strainer, water heater anode |
| HVAC | Filter replacement, AC coil cleaning |
| Tires | Pressure, tread depth, rotation |
| Exterior | Slide seals, awning inspection, caulk |
| Interior | Smoke/CO detectors, LP detector, extinguisher |
| Electrical | Shore power cord, converter, solar |
| Plumbing | Black/gray tank flush, toilet seal |

### Logging a Maintenance Session

1. Click an area to expand it
2. Check off completed items
3. Add a **note** (optional) — e.g., brand/spec used, condition found
4. The session saves automatically with today's date

### Overdue Tracking

Items past their service interval show in red. The interval for each item (e.g., every 90 days, every 365 days) determines when it turns overdue.

### Edit Mode

Click **Edit** on any area to customize checklist items — add items specific to your rig, adjust intervals, or remove items that don't apply.

### Tips

- Log maintenance immediately after completing it — the date stamp is how overdue tracking works
- Add notes for anything unusual ("found crack in lap sealant at slide seam")
- Check the Coming Due panel before any long trip
